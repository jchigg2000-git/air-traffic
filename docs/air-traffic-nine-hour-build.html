The shape of the spend

$0 — and where it really went.

The honest tell of a long agentic build is the token mix. Of ~240M tokens, 234M were cache reads — the model re-reading a big cached context turn after turn as it stacked feature on feature. Output tokens — the actual writing — were ~977K. Cost follows that shape:

Cache reads are 75% of the bill — expected, not alarming: it is the signature of an agent holding the whole project in its head. Rates from the claude-api skill; tokens deduped by message.id.

The number to distrust

~0× is an upper bound.

~100× 13,567 net lines ÷ a ~150-lines/day human assumption ≈ 90 dev-days vs 7h 3m active. Tidy arithmetic — and almost certainly too generous.

Why it overstates. A large share of those 13.6K lines is not hand-reasoned logic. One commit alone (30caaf2) is 69 files of specs, executive PDFs and brand assets — 2,798 lines. Phase 1 is, by its own commit message, "synthetic byte-identical" vendor fixtures — 3,690 lines of generated surface, not reasoned code. Subtract the generated mass and the genuine engineering shrinks hard.

What actually landed

Not a mockup. A working spec you can run.

The settled structure is a real system: it drives 16 AI vendors' admin surfaces and normalizes them into one ops-observation-batch/v1 signal across three control planes — and it is honest about what it cannot enforce.

No vendor is really contacted. Those surfaces are a synthetic replica air-traffic serves itself, on its own port — a hand-written vendor-exact fixture for seven, a labelled generic envelope for the other nine. Not one of the sixteen adapters holds a live credential or reaches the internet. What is real is the shape: the surfaces, the error envelopes, the policy engine, the emitted signal contract.
